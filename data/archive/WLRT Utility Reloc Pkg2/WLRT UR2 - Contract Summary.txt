CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Westhaven LRT - Utility Relocation Package 2 (UR2)
Owner: Ontario Transit Infrastructure Agency (OTIA)
Delivery: Design-Bid-Build
Contract Price: $15,300,000
Start: March 2022   Target: March 2024

Scope: relocate municipal and private utilities from the future LRT guideway along King Street, approx. 3 km. Includes 1.6 km of new sanitary and storm sewers, 2.1 km of watermain, hydro duct banks (in partnership with Westhaven Hydro), and coordination of Bell and gas relocations by others.

Traffic: King St stays open, minimum one lane each way, with business access maintained at all times.

PM: Daniel Okafor   Super: Luis Fernandes
