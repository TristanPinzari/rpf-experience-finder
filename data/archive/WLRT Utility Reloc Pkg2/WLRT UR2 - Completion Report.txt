COMPLETION REPORT - WESTHAVEN LRT UTILITY RELOCATION PACKAGE 2

Substantial performance: May 2024 (2 months past target due to third-party utility delays).
Final contract value: $16.7M.

Ridgeline relocated sewers, watermains and hydro duct banks along 3 km of King Street to clear the future LRT guideway. Corridor handed over to the guideway contractor in segments, the last in May 2024.

King Street stayed open throughout with at least one lane in each direction, and business access was maintained. Ridgeline coordinated daily with Bell, Enbridge and Westhaven Hydro crews working in the same corridor.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries. Lessons: third-party utility schedules must be tied to the owner's master schedule with escalation dates.
