UPPER RIDGE ROAD WIDENING - COMPLETION REPORT
Substantial completion: October 2020. Final value: $8.3M.

Built in four traffic stages over two seasons. One lane in each direction was kept open at all times. Hydro pole relocation delays in 2019 were absorbed by resequencing the storm sewer work.

New signals at three intersections were switched on with no outages. The multi-use path opened ahead of schedule in August 2020.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries.
