City of Westhaven - Transportation Services

January 2021

Ridgeline Constructors widened Upper Ridge Road for the City between 2019 and 2020. The project kept two lanes of traffic open on a busy arterial through every stage, and Ridgeline's coordination with Westhaven Hydro and other utilities was effective. Residents' concerns were handled quickly.

We recommend Ridgeline for road reconstruction under traffic.

Owen Takahashi, P.Eng., Project Manager, Transportation Capital
