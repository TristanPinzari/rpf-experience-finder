CONTRACT SUMMARY - UPPER RIDGE ROAD WIDENING
Owner: City of Westhaven, Transportation Services
Contract: WH-T-2019-02, design-bid-build
Contract price: $7,900,000
Dates: April 2019 - October 2020

Scope: widen 1.8 km of Upper Ridge Road from two to four lanes between Concession 5 and Fennel Ave. New storm sewers, curb and gutter, three signalized intersections, 3 m multi-use path, streetlighting, and utility pole relocations (by Westhaven Hydro).
Traffic: two lanes (one each way) maintained at all times; staged construction.
PM: Daniel Okafor
