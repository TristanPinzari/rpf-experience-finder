Scope Summary:
Rehabilitation of a two-span reinforced concrete overpass carrying Highway 7. Work includes partial-depth and full-depth deck patching, new waterproofing and paving, replacement of all elastomeric bearings (jacking required), removal and replacement of parapet/barrier walls, and new expansion joints at both abutments.

Traffic Staging:
Contract requires one lane in each direction to remain open at all times. Work to be completed in two stages (north half / south half) with temporary concrete barrier. Lane closures for barrier moves permitted 22:00 to 06:00 only.
