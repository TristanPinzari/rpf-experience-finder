PROJECT COMPLETION REPORT
Highway 7 Overpass Rehabilitation, Guelph   (Job 2019-031)

Summary
Ridgeline rehabilitated the two-span Highway 7 overpass for MTO over two construction seasons (May 2019 to November 2020). Work included deck patching and waterproofing, full bearing replacement, new barrier walls and expansion joints.

Completion
Substantial performance was certified on November 6, 2020, about three weeks after the original target. The delay was granted as an extension due to the ballast wall repair at the north abutment.

Cost
Final contract value and change order breakdown: see CO log rev 6 in project file. Project finished within the approved budget envelope with no outstanding claims.

Challenges
- Traffic: one lane in each direction was kept open for the entire project using two-stage construction and temporary concrete barrier. Barrier moves and lane shifts were done overnight.
- Hidden deterioration at bearing pedestals and the north ballast wall required redesign during construction.
- Joint supply delays were managed by approving an equivalent product.

Safety
Zero lost-time injuries. 41,000 person-hours worked. One recorded near miss involving traffic control; corrective action taken.

Lessons Learned
- Budget more time for pedestal condition surveys before bearing work.
- Night barrier moves need a dedicated traffic control lead.
- Early MTO coordination on lane schedule paid off.
