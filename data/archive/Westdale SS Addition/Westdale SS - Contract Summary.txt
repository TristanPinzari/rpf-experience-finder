CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Westdale Secondary School - Science Wing Addition and Facade Restoration
Owner: Ridgeview District School Board
Delivery: Construction Management (CM fee + GC)
Contract value (GMP at award): $11,500,000
Start: March 2021   Target: December 2022

Scope:
- New three-storey science wing (approx. 2,600 m2) with 9 labs, prep rooms and an elevator
- Restoration of the 1927 front facade (listed on the municipal heritage register): masonry repointing, stone repair, window restoration
- Tie-in to existing building and new main entrance

Constraints: school operates throughout. Construction zone fully hoarded with separate access off Mill Road. Heritage work requires approval of the City heritage planner for mortar, stone and window details.

PM: Sarah Lindqvist   Super: Kevin Achterberg
