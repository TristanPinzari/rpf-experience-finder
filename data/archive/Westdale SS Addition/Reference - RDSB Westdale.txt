Ridgeview District School Board

March 2, 2023

Reference: Ridgeline Constructors Ltd.

Ridgeline acted as Construction Manager for the Westdale Secondary School science wing addition and heritage facade restoration, completed in January 2023. They delivered a complex addition and sensitive heritage work on an operating secondary school with more than 1,100 students.

The Board was impressed with how Ridgeline handled heritage approvals with the City and with the quality of the masonry restoration. Safety and separation from students were well managed throughout.

Paul Novak
Senior Project Officer
