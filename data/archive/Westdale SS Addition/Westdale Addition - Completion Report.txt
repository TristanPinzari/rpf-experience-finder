WESTDALE SECONDARY SCHOOL - SCIENCE WING ADDITION
Completion report

Substantial performance: January 2023 (Jan 16, 2023)
Final value: $12.2M

The new three-storey science wing opened to students for the second semester of 2023. The 1927 heritage facade was restored with lime mortar repointing, stone repair and replacement, and restoration of 18 original wood windows, all approved by the City heritage planner.

The school stayed fully operational for the whole project. Construction was separated from students by hoarding and a separate site entrance, and crane activity was scheduled around student arrival and dismissal.

Challenges: sandstone quarry lead times and lime mortar curing in cold weather (heated enclosures used in Nov 2021). Completion slipped about one month past the December 2022 target, by agreement with the Board.

Safety: no lost-time injuries.
