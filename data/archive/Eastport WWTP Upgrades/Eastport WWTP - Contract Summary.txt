CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Eastport Wastewater Treatment Plant Upgrades (Phase 1 and Phase 2)
Owner: City of Port Calder, Water and Wastewater Services
Contract: PC-WW-2019-03   Delivery: Design-Bid-Build
Combined contract price: $22,400,000
Start: March 2019   Target completion: May 2022

Phase 1: new secondary clarifiers No. 3 and No. 4, RAS/WAS pumping station.
Phase 2: replacement of aeration blowers, new diffuser grids in aeration tanks 1-4, electrical upgrades to MCC building.

Operating requirement: the plant must stay in compliance with its Environmental Compliance Approval at all times. Tanks may be taken out of service one at a time only. Bypass pumping required for tie-ins.

PM: Ahmed Siddiqui   Super: Marie Tremblay
