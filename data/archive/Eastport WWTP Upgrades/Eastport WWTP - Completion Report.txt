COMPLETION REPORT - EASTPORT WASTEWATER TREATMENT PLANT UPGRADES

Phase 1 (clarifiers, RAS/WAS pumping) completed December 2020.
Phase 2 (aeration, blowers, electrical) completed June 2022. Substantial performance of the overall contract: June 2022.

The plant remained in service and within its compliance limits throughout both phases. Tanks were taken offline one at a time, and bypass pumping was used during all tie-ins and wet weather events.

Key challenges: rock in the clarifier excavation, deteriorated concrete in aeration tank 2, and a wet spring in 2021. The new blowers have cut the plant's aeration energy use by about 30% according to the City.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries over 3+ years.
