COMPLETION REPORT - KINGSMERE ARMOURY REHABILITATION

Substantial completion: March 2020 (extended from Dec 2019 for roof truss repairs).
Final contract value: $11.2M.

Ridgeline rehabilitated the heritage armoury for DCC and DND. Work included masonry restoration with lime mortar, stone repair and replacement, a new slate and copper roof, and full mechanical, electrical and life safety upgrades. All heritage work was reviewed and accepted by the federal heritage review office.

The armoury stayed in use by the reserve unit during construction.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries.
