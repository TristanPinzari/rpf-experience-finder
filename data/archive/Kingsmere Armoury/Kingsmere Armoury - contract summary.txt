CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Kingsmere Armoury Rehabilitation, Kingston ON
Contracting authority: Defence Construction Canada (DCC) on behalf of the Department of National Defence (DND)
Contract: DCC KI-2018-41   Delivery: design-bid-build
Contract price: $10,400,000
Start: April 2018   Target completion: December 2019

Scope: rehabilitation of a 1912 brick and limestone armoury, a Recognized Federal Heritage Building. Masonry repointing and stone repair, full roof replacement (slate and copper), new mechanical and electrical systems, and fire alarm and sprinkler upgrades.

Heritage: all work on character-defining elements reviewed by the Federal Heritage Buildings Review Office (FHBRO) consultant.

PM: Ahmed Siddiqui   Super: Marie Tremblay
