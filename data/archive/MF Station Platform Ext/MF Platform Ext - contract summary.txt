CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Millbrook Falls Station - Platform Extension
Owner: Ontario Transit Infrastructure Agency (OTIA)
Contract price: $6,200,000, design-bid-build
Start May 2021, target completion June 2022

Scope: extend the side platform by 120 m to accommodate 12-car trains. New canopy (120 m), LED lighting, tactile warning strips, PA system extension, and drainage.

Rail constraints: corridor active. Work within 4.5 m of track only during overnight track possessions (01:00 to 05:00) booked through the railway. All workers need railway safety orientation; flag protection on every shift.
