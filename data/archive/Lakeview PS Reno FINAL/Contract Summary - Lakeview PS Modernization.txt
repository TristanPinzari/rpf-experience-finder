CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Lakeview PS Modernization
Owner: Ridgeview District School Board, Facility Services
Tender: RDSB-T-19-022   Delivery: Design-Bid-Build (CCDC 2)
Contract Price: $6,800,000 excl. HST
Start: June 2019   Target completion: August 2021

Scope:
- Full HVAC replacement (rooftop units, new ventilation to all classrooms)
- Accessibility upgrades: new elevator, accessible washrooms, ramps at two entrances
- Renovation of 14 classrooms and 4 washroom groups

Phasing:
School remains in operation during the school year. Heavy work (demolition, mechanical shutdowns, elevator shaft) restricted to summer 2019 and summer 2020. During the school year work is limited to areas separated by full-height hoarding with separate entrances for workers. No noisy work 8:30 am to 3:30 pm in occupied wings.

Board requirements: all workers on site during school year need a police vulnerable sector check.

PM: Sarah Lindqvist   Superintendent: Kevin Achterberg
