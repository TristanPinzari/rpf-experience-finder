COMPLETION REPORT - LAKEVIEW PUBLIC SCHOOL RENEWAL

Client: Ridgeview District School Board
Completed: August 2021 (substantial performance Aug 24, 2021)
Final Contract Value: $7,300,000 (original plus 7 change orders)

Overview
Ridgeline delivered a full renewal of Lakeview Public School while the school remained open. Mechanical systems were replaced, an elevator and accessible washrooms were added, and classrooms were renovated.

Phasing approach
The work was carried out over two summers, when the school was closed, with lighter work during the school year behind hoarding. Students and staff were separated from the work at all times by full-height hoarding and separate site access. COVID-19 school closures in spring 2020 allowed some work to be accelerated.

Challenges
- Asbestos in pipe insulation larger than surveyed (change order).
- Noise control during class hours required strict trade coordination.
- Elevator equipment delivery delayed 6 weeks in 2021.

Safety: no lost-time incidents. No incidents involving students or staff.

Lessons learned: do the summer schedule in reverse from the September opening date; give the principal a weekly look-ahead.
