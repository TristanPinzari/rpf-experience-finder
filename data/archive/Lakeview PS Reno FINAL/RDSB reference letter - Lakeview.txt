Ridgeview District School Board
Facility Services

October 5, 2021

To whom it may concern:

Ridgeline Constructors Ltd. completed the modernization of Lakeview Public School for our Board in August 2021. The project included new HVAC, an elevator and accessibility upgrades, and classroom renovations, all while the school continued to operate.

Ridgeline's site team was respectful of our students and staff. Their phasing plan concentrated disruptive work in the summer months, and they consistently met our requirements for separation, noise and worker screening. The school opened on time in both September 2020 and September 2021.

We would recommend Ridgeline for renovation work in occupied school facilities.

Paul Novak
Senior Project Officer, Capital Projects
