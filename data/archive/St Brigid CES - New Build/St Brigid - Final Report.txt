ST. BRIGID CATHOLIC ELEMENTARY SCHOOL - FINAL PROJECT REPORT

The new school was substantially complete on August 9, 2019 and opened to students on September 3, 2019 as planned. Final contract value was $14.6M.

Highlights
- 450-pupil school with gym, library and child care wing.
- Geothermal system with 60 boreholes provides all heating and cooling.
- The building achieved LEED Silver certification (CaGBC, certified June 2020). Ridgeline managed the construction credits: construction waste diversion reached 81%, and indoor air quality management plan was followed throughout.

Challenges
Rock encountered in part of the borehole field slowed drilling. A cold winter in 2018 needed extra heat and hoarding to keep the structure on schedule.

Safety: 0 lost-time injuries, 96,000 hours.

Lessons: geotechnical investigation should cover the full borehole field, not just the building footprint.
