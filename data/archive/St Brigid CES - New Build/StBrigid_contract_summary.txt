CONTRACT SUMMARY - ST. BRIGID CATHOLIC ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
Owner: Halbury Catholic District School Board
Delivery: Design-Bid-Build, stipulated price
Contract Price: $14,200,000
Start July 2017 / Substantial completion target August 2019 (opening Sept 2019)

Scope: New two-storey school for 450 pupils (JK-8), approx. 5,400 m2. Includes double gymnasium, library, 24 classrooms, and a five-room child care wing with separate entrance. Geothermal heating and cooling with 60 boreholes. Site works include bus loop, parking for 80 cars, and play areas.

Architect: Moreau Lindsay Architects
PM: Sarah Lindqvist   Super: Tom Kowalczyk
