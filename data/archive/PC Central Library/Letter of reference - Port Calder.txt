May 15, 2024

To whom it may concern,

Re: Ridgeline Constructors Ltd. - Central Library Renewal

The City engaged Ridgeline Constructors for the renewal of the Port Calder Central Library, a project of approximately $4.6 million completed in March 2024. The renewal has transformed the building, with full accessibility, modern mechanical systems and new spaces the community has embraced.

Ridgeline's project manager, Kevin Achterberg, kept City and library staff informed throughout, and the reopening date was met.

Sincerely,
Rosa Delgado
Manager, Facility Capital Projects
