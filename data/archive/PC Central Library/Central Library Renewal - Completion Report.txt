COMPLETION REPORT - PORT CALDER CENTRAL LIBRARY RENEWAL

Substantial completion: March 2024. The library reopened to the public on April 6, 2024.

Scope delivered: interior renewal of the 3-floor central library. New mechanical systems (AHUs, controls), new elevator and accessible washrooms on all floors, 10 bookable study rooms, a makerspace with laser cutters and 3D printers, new finishes and lighting throughout.

The building was closed during construction, which simplified phasing. Key challenge was hazardous materials (asbestos in floor tile mastic) found during demolition, handled under the City's abatement contract.

Safety: zero incidents.
