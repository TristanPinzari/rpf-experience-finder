CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Credit River Pedestrian Bridge
Client: City of Westhaven, Engineering Services
Tender: WH-ENG-2021-07   Delivery: Design-Bid-Build
Contract Price: $2,100,000 + HST
Start: April 2021   Target completion: May 2022

Scope: Supply and install a 45 m prefabricated steel truss pedestrian bridge over the Credit River. Includes pile-supported concrete abutments, approach slabs, trail connections on both banks, and site restoration.

Environmental: In-water and near-water work restricted to July 1 to September 15 under the conservation authority permit. Erosion and sediment control plan required.

Key dates: Truss fabrication 16 weeks. Single crane lift planned for spring 2022.

PM: Priya Raman   Super: Tom Kowalczyk
