Credit River Pedestrian Bridge (CRPB) - Completion Report

Ridgeline completed the Credit River Pedestrian Bridge for the City of Westhaven. Substantial completion was reached in June 2022.

Work summary: Steel pipe piles were driven at both abutments during the 2021 in-water window. Concrete abutments were cast in fall 2021. The 45 m truss was delivered in two sections, spliced on site, and lifted into place with a 300-tonne crawler crane in a single day in May 2022. Trail connections and landscaping followed.

Final value: approx. $2.25M including two change orders (additional pile length, extra trail lighting).

Challenges: High river levels in early July 2021 delayed pile driving by two weeks. Crane pad required extra granular due to soft soils.

Environmental: No permit infractions. Conservation authority inspector signed off without deficiencies.

Safety: No lost-time incidents.

Lessons: lock in crane booking early; spring river levels are unpredictable.
