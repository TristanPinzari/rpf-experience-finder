To Whom It May Concern,

Re: Ridgeline Constructors Ltd. - Credit River Pedestrian Bridge

I am pleased to provide this reference for Ridgeline Constructors, who served as general contractor for the City's Credit River Pedestrian Bridge, completed in June 2022.

Ridgeline managed a technically demanding job that included in-water pile work under strict environmental permit conditions and a single-day lift of a 45-metre steel truss. The lift was planned carefully and executed without incident. The project team, led by Priya Raman, communicated clearly with City staff and with residents who use the trail system.

The bridge has become a popular connection in our trail network. We would welcome Ridgeline on future City projects.

Sincerely,

Andrea Mollins, P.Eng.
Manager, Capital Projects
City of Westhaven
