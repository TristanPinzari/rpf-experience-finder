Halton Lakeshore Hospital

July 10, 2023

To Whom It May Concern,

I am writing in support of Ridgeline Constructors Ltd., Construction Manager for our Emergency Department renovation and expansion, completed in May 2023.

This was a complex project in one of the busiest areas of our hospital. Ridgeline worked through five phases while the department remained open around the clock. Their infection prevention and control practices were excellent, and our IPAC team found them responsive and disciplined.

Ridgeline's team, led by Grace Mwangi, earned the trust of our clinical staff. I would recommend them for work in operating healthcare facilities.

Daniel Carvalho
Director, Facilities and Capital Redevelopment
