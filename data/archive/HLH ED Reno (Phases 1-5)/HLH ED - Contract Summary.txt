CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Emergency Department Renovation and Expansion
Owner: Halton Lakeshore Hospital
Delivery: Construction Management at risk (GMP)
GMP at award: $18,600,000
Start: February 2021   Target completion: March 2023

Scope:
Renovation of the existing 3,200 m2 emergency department and a 900 m2 expansion. New triage and ambulance bays, 12 new treatment spaces, two negative-pressure isolation rooms, a new mental health assessment area, and upgraded medical gas, nurse call and mechanical systems.

Phasing: five phases. The ED remains open 24/7. Each phase requires the hospital to relocate services before the construction zone is turned over.

Infection control: ICRA Class IV precautions in all phases adjacent to patient areas. Negative pressure in work zones, HEPA filtration, sticky mats, daily particle counts, and sign-off by the hospital's Infection Prevention and Control (IPAC) team before each phase starts and ends.
