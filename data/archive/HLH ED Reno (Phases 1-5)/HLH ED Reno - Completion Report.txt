PROJECT COMPLETION REPORT
Halton Lakeshore Hospital - Emergency Department Renovation

Substantial completion: May 2023
Final contract value: $19.8M

Summary
Ridgeline completed the five-phase renovation and expansion of the HLH emergency department. The ED stayed open and fully operational for the full 27-month project, seeing roughly 60,000 patient visits per year during construction.

Infection control
ICRA Class IV measures were in place throughout. IPAC signed off at the start and end of all five phases. There were no construction-related infection incidents reported by the hospital.

Challenges
- COVID-19 waves in 2021 and 2022 forced resequencing of phases 2 and 3.
- Hidden asbestos and old medical gas piping in phase 1.
- Coordinating patient moves with hospital staff before each phase.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries.

Lessons
Start each phase with an ICRA walk including the charge nurse. Build the patient relocation schedule with the hospital before pricing phases.
