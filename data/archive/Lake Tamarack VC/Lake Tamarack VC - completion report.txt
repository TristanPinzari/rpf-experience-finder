LAKE TAMARACK VISITOR CENTRE - COMPLETION REPORT
Substantial completion October 2023. Final value $5.9M.

The mass timber structure was erected in 11 working days. Remote site logistics included a temporary work camp and fuel management plan. Environmental monitors were on site during all clearing and earthworks. No environmental incidents were recorded.

Challenges: CLT panel delivery delays; road washout in April 2023 closed access for 9 days.

Safety: no lost-time injuries.
