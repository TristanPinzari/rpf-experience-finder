CONTRACT SUMMARY - LAKE TAMARACK VISITOR CENTRE
Owner: Federal Parks Agency (Government of Canada)
Contract: design-bid-build, $5,600,000
May 2022 - September 2023
Scope: new 900 m2 single-storey visitor centre: mass timber (glulam and CLT) structure, exhibit hall, washrooms, staff offices, septic system and solar array.
Site: remote park site, 40 km gravel road access. Strict environmental protection plan: no work within 30 m of the lake, species-at-risk monitoring (turtles), and no clearing during bird nesting season.
PM: Priya Raman
