Federal Parks Agency - Asset Management, Ontario Unit

January 2024

The Federal Parks Agency confirms that Ridgeline Constructors Ltd. built the new Lake Tamarack Visitor Centre, completed in October 2023. The contractor worked successfully at a remote site under a demanding environmental protection plan, including species-at-risk protocols. The mass timber building is a showcase for the park.

We consider Ridgeline a reliable contractor for federal projects.

Jean-Philippe Roy, Senior Project Manager
