DESIGN-BUILD CONTRACT SUMMARY
Owner: Regional Municipality of Halbury, Public Works - Water Services
Project: Burnside Reservoir Rehabilitation
Contract: $9,700,000 (design-build)
Start: April 2023   Target completion: August 2024

Scope: rehabilitation of the 20 ML buried concrete reservoir (two cells). New NSF-61 liner in both cells, roof slab replacement over cell 1, new valve chamber and piping, and site security upgrades.

Service continuity: one cell in service at all times. During the valve chamber work, temporary supply through a bypass main to maintain pressure to the Burnside zone.

Design engineer (sub): Kestrel Water Consulting
PM: Ahmed Siddiqui
