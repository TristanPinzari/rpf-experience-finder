Regional Municipality of Halbury - Water Services

November 2024

Re: Ridgeline Constructors Ltd.

Ridgeline completed the design-build rehabilitation of our Burnside Reservoir in September 2024. The team kept the reservoir in service throughout, and residents of the Burnside pressure zone experienced no service interruptions. Their design-build team was proactive on design decisions and communicated well with our operations staff.

We would work with Ridgeline again.

Monique Bergeron, P.Eng., Manager of Water Infrastructure
