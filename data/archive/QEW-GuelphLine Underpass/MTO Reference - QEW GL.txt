Ministry of Transportation
Central Region - Contract Management Office

December 12, 2022

Re: Reference for Ridgeline Constructors Ltd.

Ridgeline Constructors recently completed the deck replacement of the Guelph Line underpass on the QEW in Burlington, a contract of nearly $14 million. The work used accelerated bridge construction with precast deck panels, which reduced the number of lane closures needed on one of the busiest highways in the province.

Ridgeline's team maintained QEW traffic throughout three construction stages and responded well when site conditions differed from records. Their schedule recovery effort in 2022 was effective.

The Ministry rates Ridgeline's performance on this contract as satisfactory to good.

Regards,
Samuel Pereira
Senior Project Engineer
