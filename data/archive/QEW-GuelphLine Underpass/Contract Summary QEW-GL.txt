RIDGELINE CONSTRUCTORS LTD. - CONTRACT SUMMARY

Project: QEW / Guelph Line Underpass Deck Replacement
Owner: Ministry of Transportation of Ontario, Central Region
Contract: MTO 2021-2118
Location: Burlington, ON
Delivery: Design-Bid-Build
Original Contract Price: $12,700,000
Start: April 2021
Contract completion date: September 30, 2022

Scope:
Full replacement of the deck of the Guelph Line underpass carrying the QEW. Contract specifies accelerated bridge construction using precast concrete deck panels with UHPC closure joints. Includes new approach slabs, barrier walls, and drainage.

Staging:
QEW traffic maintained at all times with lane reductions in three stages. Full overnight closures of Guelph Line permitted with detour. QEW lane closures 21:00 to 05:30 only.

Staff: Daniel Okafor (PM), Luis Fernandes (Super)
