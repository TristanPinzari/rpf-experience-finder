FIRE STATION NO. 7 - COMPLETION REPORT
Substantial completion: May 2021. Station in service June 2021.
Final value: $9.2M.

Delivered a post-disaster fire station with 4 apparatus bays, dormitories and a training tower. The building achieved LEED Silver certification in 2022.

COVID-19 hit one month into construction; site protocols kept the job running. Structural steel delivery was delayed 5 weeks, recovered by double-shifting the training tower.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries.
