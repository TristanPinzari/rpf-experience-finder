CLOSEOUT REPORT - ORCHARD HILLS LONG-TERM CARE HOME

Occupancy granted April 2022, about one month late. Final contract value $28.3M.

The project ran almost entirely during the COVID-19 pandemic. Ridgeline introduced daily screening, staggered trade shifts and extra sanitation. Mechanical design was upgraded during construction to meet new provincial guidance on ventilation in LTC homes.

The home was built to the provincial LTC design standards and passed the Ministry's pre-occupancy review on the first visit. Evergreen received its provincial per-bed funding on schedule.

Safety: 1 lost-time injury (fall from ladder, 4 days lost).
Lessons: pandemic-period supply chain needs long-lead tracking from day one.
