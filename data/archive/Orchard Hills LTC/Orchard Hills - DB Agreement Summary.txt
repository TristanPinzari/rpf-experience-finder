DESIGN-BUILD AGREEMENT SUMMARY
Owner: Evergreen Seniors Living Inc.
Project: Orchard Hills Long-Term Care Home, Halbury
Contract value: $27,000,000 (design-build, fixed price)
Start: January 2020   Target occupancy: March 2022

Scope: new four-storey, 160-bed long-term care home in 5 resident home areas of 32 beds. Built to the provincial Long-Term Care Home Design Manual. Includes kitchen, laundry, chapel, and courtyard.

Funding note: Evergreen is a private operator. The project is supported by a provincial construction funding subsidy per bed, paid to Evergreen. Ridgeline's contract is with Evergreen only.

Design team (sub): Hartwell Architects
PM: Grace Mwangi   Super: Tom Kowalczyk
