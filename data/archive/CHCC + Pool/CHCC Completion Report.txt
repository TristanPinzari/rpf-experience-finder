COMPLETION REPORT - CARLISLE HEIGHTS COMMUNITY CENTRE AND AQUATIC FACILITY

Substantial completion: June 2021. Opened to the public July 2021 (after provincial COVID-19 reopening).
Final value: $21.0M.

Delivered design-build for the City of Westhaven. Facility includes an 8-lane 25 m pool, leisure pool, gymnasium, fitness centre and multipurpose rooms. LEED Gold certification was awarded in 2022, supported by rooftop solar, high-efficiency pool dehumidification with heat recovery, and 88% construction waste diversion.

Challenges: pool tank waterproofing required a second flood test; COVID-19 protocols slowed interior trades in 2020.

Safety: zero lost-time injuries.
