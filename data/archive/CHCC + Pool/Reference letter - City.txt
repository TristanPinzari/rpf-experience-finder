City of Westhaven - Recreation Services

September 2021

To whom it may concern,

Ridgeline Constructors Ltd. recently delivered a new community centre and pool for the City under a design-build contract. The project was completed during the pandemic and still opened as soon as public health rules allowed. The facility has been very well received by residents.

Ridgeline's design-build team listened to City staff and the community during design, and its sustainability work supported the City's LEED goals. We would gladly work with them again.

Regards,
Marcus Ellery
Director, Recreation Facilities
