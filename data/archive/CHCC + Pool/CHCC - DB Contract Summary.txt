DESIGN-BUILD CONTRACT SUMMARY
Project: Carlisle Heights Community Centre and Aquatic Facility
Owner: Corporation of the City of Westhaven, Recreation Services
Contract: $19,800,000 (design-build, fixed price)
Start: May 2019   Target opening: May 2021

Scope: new 6,800 m2 community centre with an 8-lane 25 m pool and leisure pool, full-size gymnasium, fitness centre, 4 multipurpose rooms, and a 220-space parking lot.
Sustainability: City council requires LEED Gold certification.

Design team (sub): Fenwick Ross Architects
PM: Sarah Lindqvist   Super: Tom Kowalczyk
